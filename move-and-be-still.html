    The reset

    Stretch & "Be Still" Breathing

    Flow straight from the movement into this 2–3 minute reset. It brings your heart rate down and shifts you from "go" to "rest."

    Breathe
    Follow the circle: in for 4, hold for 4, out for 6.
    Gentle stretches (hold each ~20–30 sec, never bounce):
    • Overhead reach — inhale, reach both arms up tall; exhale, soften.
    • Chest opener — clasp hands behind your back, gently open the chest (undoes screen-hunched posture).
    • Standing forward fold — soft knees, hang toward the floor, release the neck.
    • Gentle neck release — ear toward shoulder, each side.
    • Calf or figure-four stretch — whatever your body needs most.

    As you breathe out, let your shoulders drop. The longer exhale is what tells your body it is safe to rest. Let each exhale be a small act of trust.
