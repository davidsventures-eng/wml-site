Breath Prayer · 5 to 10 quiet minutes

Be still with the One who gave you breath.

Slow your breathing, pray one line of Scripture, and rest in His presence. No score, no hurry, nothing to finish.

“Yahweh God formed man from the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.”Genesis 2:7 (WEB)

What do you need today?

Choose a prayer. You'll breathe in on the first half and out on the second.

    How long

    Plus a one-minute settle and a minute of silence.

    Breathing pace

    Both are about six breaths a minute. If either feels strained, breathe however is comfortable.

    Sound

    A quiet tone rises as you breathe in and falls as you breathe out.

    Be still · 5 minutes
    Find a comfortable seat. You can close your eyes once the rhythm feels natural; the tone will guide you.

    How it flows

    1. SettleLook at what He made. Let your shoulders drop.
    2. Breathe the WordIn on the first half of the line, out on the second.
    3. SilenceNo words for a minute. Just rest with Him.
    4. WonderSee how He designed your breath and heart to move together.
    5. Answer HimWrite one line back to God. It stays on your device.
    Why breath and Scripture together
    • When people prayed a rhythmic prayer aloud, their breathing slowed to about six breaths a minute, and their heart rhythm and breath began moving together (BMJ, 2001).
    • Across 31 studies, slow-paced breathing lowered heart rate and blood pressure in the moment, with a smaller effect on stress (Mindfulness, 2023).
    • People who meditated on a phrase about God had less anxiety and more sense of closeness to God than people using a secular phrase (Journal of Behavioral Medicine, 2005).
    • A two-week program of the Jesus Prayer lowered daily stress for Christians compared with waiting (Spirituality in Clinical Practice, 2018).

    These studies are small, and this is a way to pray, not a medical treatment. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (US) or go to your nearest emergency room. You can also pray with us.
