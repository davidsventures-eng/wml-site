Held · 7 days

You are held.

For grief, stress, hurt and heartbreak. Each day brings one promise from Scripture, a breath prayer, a gentle walk with a guide in your ear, and a few lines to write if you want to. Go at your own pace: seven days, or seven weeks. A hard day is not a failed day.

“The LORD is near to those who have a broken heart, and saves those who have a crushed spirit.” Psalm 34:18 (WEBU)

How each day works

Listen, walk, then write.

1Press play and walkA calm voice reads the day’s promise and walks with you for about six minutes. If you can’t walk today, sit by a window and listen.
2Pray with your breathBreathe in one short line, breathe out another. A long, slow exhale helps your body settle while your heart turns to Him.
3Write a little, or not at allShort, optional prompts. Setting the pen down is allowed. Type here and it saves on your device, or print the journal and write by hand.
