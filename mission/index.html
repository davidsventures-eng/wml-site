Please read this first

Wonderfully Made Labs is not a nonprofit, at least not yet. It is a small sole-proprietor business.

Money you send us is not a donation and is not tax-deductible. When you buy a book or printable, that is a purchase. If you choose to contribute, it is a gift to a small business and is counted here as income.

If we ever form a nonprofit or partner with one, we will say so on this page first.

How we handle money

Five rules, in this order

Any money that comes in, from sales or contributions, follows these steps.

  1. 1

    First, give back to God

    The first 10% of every sale is set aside to give, before anything else is paid.

    “Honor the LORD with your substance, with the first fruits of all your increase;”

    Proverbs 3:9 (WEBU)
  2. 2

    Pay what we owe

    Running costs and taxes are paid next, on time and in full.

    “Therefore give everyone what you owe: if you owe taxes, pay taxes;”

    from Romans 13:7 (WEBU)
  3. 3

    Carry no debt

    We only spend what we already have. If something can’t be paid for, it waits.

    “Owe no one anything, except to love one another;”

    from Romans 13:8 (WEBU)
  4. 4

    Keep a small reserve

    About three months of running costs are kept so the work doesn’t stop if sales slow.

    “There is precious treasure and oil in the dwelling of the wise, but a foolish man swallows it up.”

    Proverbs 21:20 (WEBU)
  5. 5

    Give away the rest

    Anything beyond that goes out as resources for people in hard seasons.

    “For the poor will never cease out of the land. Therefore I command you to surely open your hand to your brother, to your needy, and to your poor, in your land.”

    Deuteronomy 15:11 (WEBU)

Where the giving goes

Resources in bulk, for people in hard seasons

Once sales cover the cost of running, the giving share buys books, journals and printed resources in bulk for groups that walk with people in hard seasons. Each gift will be listed here with what was sent and where it went (the kind of group, not names).

Who we hope to serve

  • Grief groups and hospice teams
  • Hospital chaplains and children’s hospitals
  • Churches and pregnancy centers

What we will post

  • What was bought, and what it cost
  • What kind of group received it
  • The date it was sent
