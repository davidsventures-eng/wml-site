Our story

Made by a scientist who found peace on his knees

For over twenty years he studied the body as a molecular pathologist and carried life on his own. Then his own heart caught up with him in a hospital bed, and one night reading the Gospel of John he met a peace he had never known. Wonderfully Made Labs exists for families walking that same road: frightened, worn down, and hoping for something both true and kind.
