Who is Jesus?

He is the way home to God.

You don’t have to clean up first or find the right words. Here is the good news, told plainly from the Bible in four short steps. Take your time.

Begin reading I’m ready to follow Him

“Behold, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, then I will come in to him, and will dine with him, and he with me.” Revelation 3:20 (WEB)

1

God made you and loves you

You were made on purpose.

The same God who designed every heartbeat, every breath, and every cell in your body made you, knows you, and loves you. That love is not a feeling you have to earn. It is who He is.

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.”John 3:16 (WEB)
2

Something is broken

We can’t fix it on our own.

Like a body that has forgotten how to heal, something in every one of us has turned away from God. The Bible calls it sin. It separates us from Him, and no amount of trying harder can close that gap.

“For the wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.”Romans 6:23 (WEB)
3

Jesus came for you

He took our place.

Jesus, the Son of God, lived the life we couldn’t, died on the cross for our sins, and rose from the grave on the third day. He didn’t wait for us to be good. He came while we were still far away.

“But God commends his own love toward us, in that while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.”Romans 5:8 (WEB)
“But he was pierced for our transgressions. He was crushed for our iniquities. The punishment that brought our peace was on him; and by his wounds we are healed.”Isaiah 53:5 (WEB)
4

You can receive Him today

It is a gift, not a test.

You don’t earn this. You receive it, the way an open hand receives a gift. Turn to Jesus, trust Him, and tell Him so.

“for by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God, not of works, that no one would boast.”Ephesians 2:8–9 (WEB)
“that if you will confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord, and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.”Romans 10:9 (WEB)

If you want to follow Jesus

Tell Him, in your own words or these.

There are no magic words. God hears the heart. If it helps, you can pray this quietly, right where you are.

Lord Jesus, I need You. I have sinned, and I cannot fix myself. I believe You died for me and that God raised You from the dead. I turn to You now and receive You as my Lord and Savior. Thank You for forgiving me and making me new. Help me follow You all my days. Amen.A prayer you can pray (not a Bible verse)
I still have questions

Welcome to the family of God.

“Therefore if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old things have passed away. Behold, all things have become new.”2 Corinthians 5:17 (WEB)

Here are a few first steps. Go gently. He is patient with you.

  1. Read the Gospel of JohnOne chapter a day. It is where our founder met Jesus. Read John 1 in the World English Bible.
  2. Talk with Him every dayStart with a 3-minute Breath Prayer or the 31-Day Prayer Companion.
  3. Find a church familyLook for a church that teaches the Bible and loves Jesus. Tell the pastor you have decided to follow Him and ask about baptism.
  4. Tell someoneShare the news with a friend who follows Jesus, or tell us. We would love to pray for you by name.

Ask us to pray for you

You don’t have to carry this alone.

Tell us you said yes to Jesus, share a need, or ask a question about faith. We read every message and pray over each one. Your name and email are optional.

“Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest.”Matthew 11:28 (WEB)
What would you like to share?

We will never share or publish what you send.

If you are in danger or thinking about ending your life, please call or text 988 (US) or your local emergency number now. You matter to God, and to us.

Honest questions

It’s okay to wonder.

Do I have to be good enough first?

No. No one is. That is the whole point of grace. Jesus said He came for the sick, not the healthy. You come as you are, and He does the changing.

I used to believe, but I drifted away.

He has not moved. You can come back today, with no lecture waiting at the door.

“He who comes to me I will in no way throw out.”from John 6:37 (WEB)
What if I still have doubts?

Bring them to Him. Many of the people in the Bible did. Keep reading the Gospel of John, keep asking honest questions, and send us yours.

“You shall seek me, and find me, when you search for me with all your heart.”Jeremiah 29:13 (WEB)
Doesn’t science conflict with faith?

Our founder spent more than twenty years as a molecular pathologist. The deeper he looked into the body, the more design and care he saw. Science shows us how; it points us to the One who made it.

“The heavens declare the glory of God. The expanse shows his handiwork.”Psalm 19:1 (WEB)
