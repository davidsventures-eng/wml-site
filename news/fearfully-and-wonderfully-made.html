New book · Ages 4–9

Our First Book Is Here: Fearfully and Wonderfully Made

A Scripture & Body Science Coloring Book, now in paperback on Amazon.

Did you know your heart beats about 100,000 times every day, even while you sleep? Or that your body is made of about 30 trillion tiny living cells, and every one of them has a job to do?

Facts like these are what our first paperback is about. Fearfully and Wonderfully Made: A Scripture & Body Science Coloring Book is now available on Amazon.

Each of the 12 large coloring pages pairs one Bible verse with one true, simple fact about the body God made. Kids color alongside Iris, Sunny, Echo, Tempo and the Furnace Friend, the Wonderfully Made Labs team. Each friend shows a different way to serve God by caring for others.

Get the paperback on Amazon · $9.99 Free sample pages

Inside the book

  • 12 single-sided coloring pages with a blank back, so markers don't bleed through
  • A Bible verse on every page, quoted word for word from the World English Bible
  • A "Did you know?" science fact on every page, checked against sources like the NIH, CDC and American Academy of Sleep Medicine, with a list of sources at the back
  • A "This book belongs to" page, a note for grown-ups, a meet-the-team page and a thank-you prayer page

How to use it

As your child colors, read the verse out loud, talk about the fact, and ask one question: "What does this show us about our Creator?"
