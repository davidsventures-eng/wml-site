New Paperback: When I'm Scared, God Is With Me

A Kids' Faith & Feelings Journal, now in paperback on Amazon.

Why does your heart pound when you're scared?

God built an alarm system into your body. It's quick, strong, and designed to protect you. Our new paperback journal, When I'm Scared, God Is With Me, helps kids ages 6–11 understand that alarm, name their big feelings, and bring them to the God who is near.

“When I am afraid, I will put my trust in you.”— Psalm 56:3 (World English Bible)
Get the paperback on Amazon · $9.99 Printable edition in the shop

Inside the journal

  • Kid-sized body science: what the amygdala, adrenaline and cortisol do when we feel afraid, and what fight, flight and freeze mean
  • Words for big feelings, so children can name what they feel
  • Bible verses for fear and worry, quoted from the World English Bible
  • The Prayer Breath, a simple calming prayer kids can use anywhere
  • Daily check-ins, drawing pages and writing space, with Tempo and Iris along for the journey

How to use it

Sit with your child for a few minutes a day. Read one page together, try the Prayer Breath, and ask: "Where do you feel it in your body, and what can we tell God about it?"
