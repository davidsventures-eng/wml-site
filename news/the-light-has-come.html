New for Advent: The Light Has Come

A 25-Day Advent Coloring Devotional, now in paperback on Amazon.

Did you know sunlight takes about 8 minutes to travel from the sun to you? On the very first day of creation, God spoke light into being, and on that first Christmas, the true Light came into the world.

The Light Has Come: A 25-Day Advent Coloring Devotional walks kids from creation to the manger, one day at a time. It is now available in paperback on Amazon.

Each of the 25 days, plus a bonus day, pairs a Bible verse with a short devotion, a one-line prayer, a coloring page and one true wonder about the world God made. Iris, Sunny, Echo and Tempo color their way through the season with your family.

Inside the book

  • 25 daily readings plus a bonus day, each with a coloring page
  • A Bible verse every day, quoted word for word from the World English Bible
  • A true wonder fact every day, simplified for kids
  • A short devotion and a one-line prayer to read together
  • An Advent star chart to color in as the days count down

How to use it

Advent begins Sunday, November 29. Each day, read the verse and the devotion together, pray the prayer, color the page, then color one star on the chart.
